# Gong 2019 — Temperature-dependent anisotropy of Nd₂Fe₁₄B from atomistic spin models

- **Citation**: Q. Gong et al., *Calculating temperature-dependent properties of
  Nd₂Fe₁₄B permanent magnets by atomistic spin model simulations*.
- **DOI / URL**: arXiv:1902.05636
- **Local PDF**: `papers/gong2019.pdf` (gitignored)
- **Tags**: constrained Monte Carlo, Nd₂Fe₁₄B, K1–K3

## Summary

Obtains the free energy from the constrained-Monte-Carlo torque
(`F = −∫ T_y dθ`, their Eq. (8)) and fits the anisotropy constants K1–K3.

## Relevance to this package

A worked example of the post-processing the CMC guide describes (torque
integration and fitting); a reference point for the Nd₂Fe₁₄B smoke run.
